READING OF THE RESULT
  Both configurations met the deadline: the detector already runs in its own
  thread and the loop reads only the latest result, so the in-loop cost is
  effectively identical (0.013 vs 0.018 ms). The difference is the cost of
  meeting it -- CPU 51.0 % -> 19.4 %, thermal rise 5x lower, jitter 36 % lower,
  and detection rate 12.3/s -> 30/s so every frame is inspected rather than
  about two in five.
